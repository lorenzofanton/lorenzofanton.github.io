Welcome! I am a PhD student in [Economics, Statistics and Data Science](https://www.dems.unimib.it/en/programmes/post-lauream/phd-economics-statistics-and-data-science) at the [University of Milano-Bicocca](https://www.unimib.it/)

My research interest include macroeconomics with a particular focus on monetary policy and DSGE models.

I hold a MSc in [Economics and Political Science]((https://eps.cdl.unimi.it/en) from the [University of Milan](https://www.unimi.it/it) and a BA in [Economia e Management](https://ema.cdl.unimi.it/it) from the [University of Milan](https://www.unimi.it/it).
